## Summary

This source provides an overview of attention in psychology, including its definition, types, neural basis, and key references from foundational works.

## Key Claims

- Attention is the selective concentration on discrete aspects of information while ignoring others.
  Evidence: Definition from cognitive psychology.
  Confidence: high

- Types include sustained, selective, and divided attention.
  Evidence: Standard categorization in the field.
  Confidence: high

- Involves prefrontal cortex, parietal cortex, and thalamus.
  Evidence: Neuroscientific consensus.
  Confidence: medium
