## Definition

Selective attention is the ability to focus on one stimulus or task while ignoring irrelevant information.

## Why It Matters

It enables humans to reduce distraction and process task-relevant information efficiently.

## Supporting Evidence

- Claim: Selective attention acts as a cognitive filter.
  Source: [[sources/attention-psychology]]
  Confidence: medium

## Competing Views

- Alternative accounts treat attention as an emergent property of neural competition.
