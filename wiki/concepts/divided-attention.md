## Definition

Divided attention is the capacity to attend to two or more tasks or streams of information at once.

## Why It Matters

It explains why multitasking often reduces performance and why task switching incurs costs.

## Supporting Evidence

- Claim: Divided attention is limited by cognitive resources.
  Source: [[sources/attention-psychology]]
  Confidence: medium

## Competing Views

- Some research suggests that automatic tasks can be handled with minimal attention.
