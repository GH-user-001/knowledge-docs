## Definition

The prefrontal cortex is a brain region involved in executive control, planning, and attention regulation.

## Why It Matters

It supports goal-directed behavior and the ability to maintain focus amid competing demands.

## Supporting Evidence

- Claim: Prefrontal cortex activity is linked to sustained attention.
  Source: [[sources/attention-psychology]]
  Confidence: medium

## Competing Views

- Other brain regions such as the parietal cortex also contribute strongly to attention.
