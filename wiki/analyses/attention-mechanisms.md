## Question

What are the main mechanisms that support human attention?

## Short Answer

Human attention involves cognitive control systems, sensory filtering, and sustained monitoring networks that work together to keep focus on task-relevant information.

## Detailed Synthesis

- Sustained attention is supported by networks linking prefrontal and parietal cortices.
- Selective attention uses filtering mechanisms to suppress irrelevant stimuli.
- Divided attention reflects the capacity limits of parallel processing when two tasks compete for resources.
